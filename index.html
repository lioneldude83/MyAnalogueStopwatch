MyChronoPro

A precision stopwatch and flexible countdown timer for workouts, intervals, focused sessions, and everyday routines.

Stopwatch and Timer in One App

MyChronoPro combines an expressive analog stopwatch with a customizable countdown timer. Track laps, save session history, create timer presets, and follow active sessions from Live Activities and Dynamic Island on supported devices.

Core Stopwatch and Timer features work without an internet connection. Your stopwatch records, timer presets, active timer state, session history, and preferences are stored locally on your device and are not synchronized through CloudKit.

Stopwatch Features

  • Large analog and digital stopwatch displays
  • Precision lap and split-time tracking
  • Automatic fastest- and slowest-lap highlights
  • Saved stopwatch session history
  • Copy lap results or export them as CSV
  • Customizable colors, hands, appearance, and app icons

Timer Features

  • Countdown durations from one second to six hours
  • Interactive minute-and-second dial
  • Create, rename, reorder, edit, and delete timer presets
  • Start, pause, resume, cancel, and stop controls
  • System-managed timer alerts powered by AlarmKit
  • Live Activities and Dynamic Island controls on supported devices

Private by Design

  • No account or sign-in required
  • No advertising or analytics trackers
  • No cross-app or cross-website tracking
  • No personal data sent to developer-operated servers
  • No CloudKit synchronization
