Privacy Policy for MyChronoPro

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

MyChronoPro respects your privacy. The developer does not collect, transmit, sell, or use your personal data for advertising, analytics, tracking, or profiling.

Data Collection

MyChronoPro does not collect personal or usage data. In particular:

  • No user account or sign-in is required.
  • No analytics or advertising trackers are included.
  • No personal data is sent to servers operated by the developer.
  • No data is sold or shared with data brokers or advertisers.
  • No cross-app or cross-website tracking is performed.

Data Stored on Your Device

MyChronoPro stores information locally on your device so that its Stopwatch and Timer features can function. This may include:

  • Stopwatch state and lap records
  • Saved stopwatch session history
  • Timer presets and their ordering
  • The state of an active timer
  • Appearance, app icon, haptic, and other app preferences

This locally stored information is not accessible to the developer and is not uploaded to a developer-operated service.

AlarmKit and Live Activities

MyChronoPro uses Apple system features, including AlarmKit, Live Activities, and Dynamic Island, to schedule timer alerts and display an active stopwatch or timer outside the app.

When you start your first timer, iOS may ask for permission to schedule alarms. This permission is used only to provide the timer functionality you request. Alarm presentation, sound, authorization, and Live Activity behavior are managed by iOS.

You can review or change alarm and Live Activity access through the iOS Settings app. MyChronoPro does not use these permissions to collect or track personal information.

Exports and User-Initiated Sharing

MyChronoPro allows you to copy stopwatch results or export lap history as a CSV file. Exporting or sharing occurs only when you initiate the action.

If you choose to share exported information with another app, service, or person, that destination may handle the information under its own privacy policy. MyChronoPro does not automatically upload exported information.

App Store Purchases

If you make an optional purchase through the App Store, the transaction is processed by Apple. The developer does not receive or store your payment-card or billing details. Apple's handling of transaction data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

MyChronoPro does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Apple system services used by the app, such as AlarmKit, Live Activities, and StoreKit, are provided and managed by Apple.

Data Retention and Deletion

Stopwatch history and timer presets remain on your device until you delete them in the app or remove the app and its associated local data. Because the developer does not receive or maintain a copy of this data, there is no developer-operated account or server record to delete.

Internet Connection

No internet connection is required to use MyChronoPro’s Stopwatch or Timer features. Stopwatch records, timer presets, active timer state, session history, and preferences are stored locally on your device and are not synchronized through CloudKit.

An internet connection may be required only when accessing Apple-operated services, such as completing an optional App Store purchase.

Children's Privacy

MyChronoPro does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if MyChronoPro's features or data practices change. The updated policy and effective date will be published on this page.
